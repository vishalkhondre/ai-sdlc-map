# Review: Lifecycle section, part one (edition 1.5.0, D-029)

## Scope

The first of two Lifecycle editions (section plan `project/plans/lifecycle.md`, D-029), reviewed
against base commit `c3c3551`. Twelve reference pages in `content/pages/lifecycle/`, in reading
order: lifecycle levels (portfolio, train and team), the SAFe reference model, the seven phase pages
(idea, specification, design, develop, build and QA, deploy, release, operate), Definition of Ready
and Done, the traceability spine, and Inspect and Adapt. With them: the D-015 relabel of the map's
lifecycle band (generic portfolio, train and team levels; "each planning cycle" for Inspect & Adapt),
new map links for the three levels, the nine phases, the DoR and DoD tags, the traceability spine and
Inspect & Adapt (`content/diagrams/map/links.yml`), the "Understand" route on the home page, the new
glossary and reference entries, and the Core back-links under a D-011 confirmation review.

## Limitations

- The seven workflow pages are not part of this edition; the map's seven workflow labels keep their
  links to the workflow catalog until edition 1.6.0 (D-029).
- Band 4 of the map keeps its SAFe words (Agile Release Train, PI cadence, system demo) until the
  enablement section, so the map mixes the generic level labels with SAFe vocabulary meanwhile
  (plan, open question 6).
- The browser check runs axe without the WCAG 2.2 rules, so the 24 px minimum target size of the
  DoR and DoD tags, the traceability spine label and the Inspect & Adapt label is met by invisible
  hit areas in the map source and checked by review, not by an automated rule.
- The confidentiality reviewer cannot see the source library. Its blocking findings rest on
  published text that the D-030 catalog diagnostic recorded as close or borderline (plan (g)1), not
  on a comparison with the library; only the library sweep compares page text with the library.
- Claims that rest on sources a session cannot read (ISO, IEC and IEEE standards, SAFe text behind
  the login wall, book content beyond a table of contents or a freely readable excerpt) are left
  off the pages, per `project/handover/KICKOFF.md` part (b).
