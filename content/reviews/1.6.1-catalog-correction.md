# Review: workflow catalog correction (edition 1.6.1)

## Scope

A removal-only correction to the workflow catalog. A diagnostic library sweep of the catalog's
structure (2026-11-28) found that two of the reference models behind the catalog's traditional map
follow the structure of material in the source library, and that three others could not be compared
because their originals are not readable through the connector (GR-1.2; unverified treated as close,
GR-6.2). This edition removes the traditional map and everything built on it: the `sources` block,
the `traditional` fields of every workflow row and the `traditional_map` block of
`content/workflows/catalog.yml`; the Traditional map view, the "was" line on each card, the
Traditional counterpart table column and detail-panel box (`site/assets/app.js`, `site/assets/style.css`);
the stat, the sources note and the traditional CSV columns (`site/generate.py`); the one-to-one mapping
sentence on the catalog page and the home page card; and one sentence on the Release page. Tests and
the browser check drop the removed view; both snapshots are refreshed. README updated.

## Limitations

- The 27 close passages logged in `project/STATUS.md` (catalog row fields, Core passages, two glossary
  entries) are not changed here; they need fresh-author rewrites and go in the next fix edition.
- Workflow notes that named a removed source model, referred to a traditional counterpart or used
  house vocabulary are deleted, not rewritten (W01, W03, W05, W06, W11, W12, W14, W17, W20, W21, W25,
  W27, W31, W34); any replacement note needs a fresh author and a library sweep in the next fix edition.
