## Suggestions (non-blocking)

1. **"This series" in glossary definitions now sits on a different site (GR-4.2).** Seven entries in `content/glossary.yml` (lines 20, 34, 47, 72, 97, 115) say "This series …" or "In this series …". Line 97 also refers to "(Part 3)" without a link. On this site the glossary lede names the series, so the text can still be understood. In a future edition, consider rewording to "the series" or "here", and linking or naming the part. The same text reaches `llms-full.txt` and the search index.
2. **Bare part references in the catalog (GR-4.2, predates).** `content/workflows/catalog.yml` lines 436 ("The Part 5 example.") and 524 ("(Part 6)") appear in the workflow detail panel with no link to the series. Consider "Part 5 of the series" with a link, or drop the reference.
3. **Deny-list coverage (GR-1.1, mechanism).** Matching is exact-case and single-word. Lower-case or all-caps spellings of a protected name, and multi-word names, are not detected unless they are hashed separately. Consider adding case-folded hashes (hash `word.lower()` and store lower-cased entries) and hashing the common variants.
4. **Earlier suggestion 3 is now more visible (GR-2.3).** The adoption-path `<desc>` and footer still say that skills, agents and platforms added early "tend to become sprawl". This reads as a finding without a source. The diagram is now inline on the home page, so the recommendation wording suggested in the 1.1.0 review is worth applying in the next edition.
5. **Leftover series identity in tooling (cosmetic).** `site/assets/app.js` line 1 still says "Beyond Faster Coding — progressive enhancement", and the theme key is `bfc-theme`. Neither is page text, but the JS file is served. The tooltip label for `common` in `app.js` ("Common usage") also differs from the page label in `generate.py` ("Common engineering usage").
6. **Relative links on the 404 page (GR-4.3, predates).** GitHub Pages serves `404.html` for missing nested paths as well. Its navigation, stylesheet and script use relative paths, which break at nested depths. The "Start from the map" link is already absolute; consider making the others absolute or adding a `<base href>`.
7. **Carried over from the 1.1.0 review, still open:** suggestions 1, 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 there (SAFe credit wording, a visible DORA/SOC 2 credit, glossary entries for SAFe and Scrum terms, the `aicpa-soc2` note wording, `accessed` fields on older references, `role="img"` in the standalone SVGs), and the manual URL checks it listed.

Verdict: ACCEPT
