# Review: Lifecycle section, part two (edition 1.6.0, D-029)

## Scope

The second of two Lifecycle editions (section plan `project/plans/lifecycle.md`, D-029). Seven
workflow pages in `content/pages/lifecycle/`, in reading order: intake and triage, specification
readiness, design conformance, pull-request verification, deploy verification, release readiness and
incident feedback. Each states its own concept and links its workflow catalog row (W01, W04, W08,
W23, W26, W25 and W28) for what the row already says (D-030). With them: the map's seven workflow
labels move from the catalog to the pages (`content/diagrams/map/links.yml`), the "Run a workflow"
route on the home page, the table of contents and the 1.6 release snapshot, the browser check's
updated map-link test, five new glossary entries, thirteen new references and note corrections to
existing references read again at their sources, and link-only edits to seven phase pages (idea,
specification, design, develop, build and QA, deploy, release, operate) and three Core pages
(workflows, software factory, harness engineering) under a D-011 confirmation review.

## Limitations

- The workflow catalog rows are not changed by this edition (D-030). Four of the seven rows the pages
  link, W01, W08, W25 and W28, carry 1.4.x findings from the D-030 catalog diagnostic, and W04, W23
  and W26 are listed there as borderline; they wait for the separate 1.4.x fix edition, so the pages
  link to rows that will change.
- The link-only edits to the phase and Core pages stay outside the passages `project/STATUS.md` lists
  as close or borderline; links that would fall inside them go under Related instead. The optional
  links in Definition of Ready and Done, the traceability spine, Inspect and Adapt, lifecycle levels
  and the evidence schema, and any edit to the SAFe reference model page, are not made.
- Claims that rest on sources a session cannot read (ISO, IEC and IEEE standards, SAFe text behind
  the login wall, book content beyond a table of contents or a freely readable excerpt) are left
  off the pages, per `project/handover/KICKOFF.md` part (b).
- The confidentiality reviewer cannot see the source library. Only the library sweep compares page
  text, glossary entries and reference notes with the library.
- No new diagram: the seven pages are prose and tables only, and the map changes only the targets of
  existing labels.
