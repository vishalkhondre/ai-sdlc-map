## Suggestions (non-blocking)

- **S1. Links inside `role="img"`.** The inline map is emitted as `<svg ... role="img" aria-labelledby="cap-ai-sdlc-map-1">`, and ARIA treats the children of `img` as presentational. Some screen readers may therefore flatten the 18 links, or announce a focused link without its role and name, even though they are keyboard-focusable. Playwright's role query finding them does not prove what a screen reader will do. Consider having `svg_source` emit `role="group"` (keeping `aria-labelledby` and the `<desc>`) when a diagram contains links, or run an axe `nested-interactive` check in `check_browser.py`. The routing row and text alternative mean nothing is unreachable today, so this does not block.
- **S2. "Dependency / CVE fix" → W15.** W15 is the right home for the dependency half, and its checks include a vulnerability scan. W29 Vulnerability response ("Are we exposed, and is the patch verified?") covers the CVE half more directly. The current link is not wrong. A later edition could link W29 from the text alternative or the catalog entry instead.
- **S3. CHANGELOG wording "the two flows that have a workflow of their own."** "Migration · rollback" could arguably be matched to W16 Data change execution ("Is this migration safe to run, and can it be undone?") or W10. The map flow is broader than data migration, so leaving it plain is defensible. The phrase still implies no other flow has a workflow. Two options:
  - reword to "the two flows with a matching workflow in the catalog", or
  - link "Migration · rollback" to W16 in a later edition.
- **S4. Caption precision.** "links to the page that covers them" is loose for the adoption stages, which lead to a section of the same page, and for the lifecycle chips, which open a catalog entry. "Underlined labels are links to where each is covered" would be exact. This is optional.
- **S5.** The intro sentence "Underlined labels open the page that covers them; or start by purpose below the map." reads awkwardly after the semicolon. Something like "…cover them. To start by purpose, use the routes below the map." would read better.
- **S6.** `rel="noopener"` on links without `target="_blank"` has no effect. It is harmless and consistent with the rest of the site.

Verdict: ACCEPT
